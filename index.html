How to play

  • Move anywhere in the arena — WASD / arrow keys, or the left thumb-stick on touch.
  • Attack with 4 moves — J K L ; or 1–4, or tap the move buttons. Moves auto-aim at your opponent.
  • Dodge! White circles mark where an attack will land. Beams charge before they fire.
  • Types matter. Super-effective hits deal double damage. Matching your monster's type adds a bonus.
  • Status: PAR slows and may freeze you in place, BRN burns and weakens, FRZ locks you briefly.
  • Knock out all 6 enemy monsters to win. P/Esc pauses, M mutes.

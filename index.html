How to play

  • Move anywhere in the arena — WASD / arrow keys, or the left thumb-stick on touch.
  • Attack with 4 moves — J K L ; or 1–4, or tap the move buttons. Moves auto-aim at your opponent.
  • Swap monsters mid-fight with the SWAP button or Q. The battle keeps going while your next monster is thrown in, and swapping has an 8 second cooldown.
  • Dodge! White circles mark where an attack will land. Beams charge before they fire.
  • Types matter. Super-effective hits deal double damage. Matching your monster's type adds a bonus.
  • Status: PAR slows and may freeze you in place, BRN burns and weakens, PSN wears you down, FRZ locks you briefly.
  • Online: one player hosts and gets a 5-letter code, the other joins with it. Each of you sees the arena from your own side.
  • 2 Players on one keyboard: Player 1 moves with WASD, attacks with R T F G and swaps with Q. Player 2 moves with the arrow keys, attacks with I O K L (or numpad 1–4) and swaps with P. On a phone or tablet, Player 2 sits across the table and gets their own controls. Gamepads work too.
    • Esc pauses (also P in 1P), M mutes.
